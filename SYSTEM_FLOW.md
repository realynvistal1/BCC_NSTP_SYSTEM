# BCC NSTP System Flow (matched to the uploaded old system)

## 1. Student
1. Student opens Enrollment and completes Academic Information, Personal Information, Physical/Health Information, Account Setup, and submission.
2. The system creates the student account and an MS/CWTS enrollment record with Pending status.
3. Student signs in through the Student Portal.
4. Dashboard shows Enrollment Status, Apply Enrollment, My Platoon, Attendance, Grades, Serial Number, and Settings.
5. After admin approval, ROTC/CWTS assignment becomes available.
6. Student marks attendance only for the correct program/MS level and within the Director's allowed geolocation radius and time window.
7. Student sees grades after the admin encodes them.
8. Student receives the NSTP serial number after completion.
9. Approved MS 1 students may apply for MS 2 during an open schedule.
10. ROTC Advance Course students may request withdrawal for ROTC Admin review.

## 2. ROTC Admin
1. Create MS 1/MS 2 enrollment schedules.
2. Review ROTC enrollment records and approve/reject applications.
3. Special preferences are handled first: Advance Course (HQ), Medics, Military Police.
4. Run Automatic Assignment for the remaining approved ROTC students.
5. Manage Battalion/Company/Platoon roster and Special Platoon.
6. Review attendance summaries for Overall, Battalion 1, Battalion 2, Advance Course, and Special Platoon.
7. Encode grades using the original 1.00-5.00 grading scale.
8. Manage attendance offenses, serial numbers, records, withdrawal requests, and settings.

## 3. CWTS Admin
1. Create CWTS 1/CWTS 2 enrollment schedules.
2. Review CWTS enrollment records and approve/reject applications.
3. Approved CWTS students are assigned to the least-loaded company (Alpha-Foxtrot).
4. Company List shows current assignments.
5. Review attendance summary.
6. Encode grades using the original 1.00-5.00 grading scale.
7. Manage attendance offenses, serial numbers, records, and settings.

## 4. NSTP Director / Officer
1. View ROTC Battalion 1, Battalion 2, Advance Course, Special Platoon, and CWTS rosters.
2. Create geolocation attendance sessions with program, MS level, MI number/type, open/close time, coordinates, and radius.
3. Sessions automatically become Scheduled, Open, or Closed based on time.
4. View attendance by program/group and see all eligible approved students, including those not yet recorded.
5. Update attendance status to Present, Late, or Absent.
6. View consolidated student records and settings.
